# M&A IT Integration Runbook

A working playbook for IT integration during mergers and acquisitions, based on nine acquisitions across the UK, Netherlands, Sweden, Germany, Portugal, and Turkey.

## Why this exists

Most M&A IT integration content is written by consultants pitching for work or by vendors selling tooling. There's very little public material from operators who've actually done the integrations and lived with the results.

This repo is my attempt to fix that gap, at least for myself. It's the playbook I wish I'd had on day one of my first integration. The structure follows the actual timeline of an acquisition: due diligence, pre-close, day one, the first 90 days, and the longer-term work that determines whether the integration was actually successful or just appeared to be.

## Who it's for

IT Managers, Heads of IT, and IT Directors at acquisitive companies, particularly those operating across multiple countries or jurisdictions. Some of the content will also be useful for advisers, integration consultants, and PE operating partners who want a practitioner's view rather than a slide deck.

## How to use it

Read 00-overview.md first. After that, the folders are numbered in the order you'd encounter the work during a real integration. Each section is short by design. The aim is operational usefulness, not comprehensiveness.

The templates folder contains stripped-down versions of documents I've used in real integrations, with confidential content removed.

## A note on scope

This repo focuses on IT integration specifically. It doesn't cover commercial integration, HR harmonisation, finance consolidation, or the broader integration management office function, except where those areas intersect with IT decisions. There are better resources for the wider integration question.
