# Stakeholder RACI Template

## What this is for

A stakeholder RACI is a simple matrix showing who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each major decision or workstream in the integration. It exists to prevent the most common failure mode in integration governance: the moment when a decision needs to be made and nobody is sure whose decision it is.

RACI matrices are sometimes overused. For a simple integration, a stakeholder RACI may be heavier than the situation warrants. For complex integrations involving multiple workstreams, multiple stakeholders, and sensitive cross-functional decisions, a clear RACI prevents weeks of avoidable confusion.

## How to read RACI

**Responsible.** The people who do the work to deliver the outcome. There can be more than one Responsible per item.

**Accountable.** The single person ultimately answerable for the outcome being delivered correctly. There must be exactly one Accountable per item. If there's more than one, accountability is unclear and the framework isn't doing its job.

**Consulted.** People whose input must be sought before the decision is made or the work proceeds. They have right of input but not right of vet
